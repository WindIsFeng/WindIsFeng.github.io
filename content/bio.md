I am a fourth-year PhD student at City University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on typhoon wind hazard modeling and risk analysis, combining AI models, probabilistic models, and physics-based models.

Before joining CityU, I pursued PhD studies in Wind Engineering at the National Wind Institute, Texas Tech University, from 2020 to 2022. I was unable to complete the program due to U.S. visa restrictions. I received my master's degree in Civil Engineering from Tongji University and my bachelor's degree from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

My long-term goal is to build reliable natural hazard prediction and decision-support models that help protect communities and save lives.
